Method

what is measured, what is computed, what is refused

The discriminant

Every usage response carries the weekly window's announced reset_at. An ordinary rollover is therefore predictable. A bonus reset is not:

quota rises AND now < previous reset_at
→ BONUS RESET

quota rises AND now ≥ previous reset_at
→ ordinary rollover, ignored

This is deterministic. No language model reads a tweet to produce Track A.

Why tweets are advisory

A post is a claim about an event, not the event. When a rollout is staged, or when nothing is announced, an instrument built on posts is blind and cannot know it. Social signals are kept out of the discriminant so that detection lag and recall remain measurable against something objective.

Credentials

The collector runs on your own machine and reads your own token. It uploads a signed, anonymous event — never a token, an account id, or a prompt. The server cannot leak what it never holds.

Refusals

Below three observed events, no risk figure is published at all — the field reads INSUFFICIENT DATA rather than a confident-looking number.

If the upstream response stops matching its expected shape, every inference halts and the field says so. Silently continuing to serve numbers over a drifted schema is the failure this instrument exists to avoid.

Claimed here: detection lag, recall, calibration. Not claimed: advance warning of any particular reset.
